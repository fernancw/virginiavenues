The Anthem

Washington, D.C.

About The Anthem

The Anthem is one of Washington D.C.’s premier music venues, located at The Wharf along the Potomac River. Opened in 2017, it’s known for its incredible acoustics, modern architecture, and flexible capacity that accommodates both intimate and large-scale shows. The venue attracts major touring artists and offers a polished, immersive concert experience.

Pros & Cons

Pros
  • Exceptional sound quality throughout the venue.
  • Modern layout with great visibility from most spots.
  • Located at The Wharf — lots of food and activities nearby.
  • Efficient staff and smooth entry process.
  • Spacious design prevents overcrowding during shows.
Cons
  • Drinks and concessions can be expensive.
  • Parking at The Wharf is limited and pricey.
  • Some standing-room sections fill up quickly.
  • Large crowds can make post-show exits slow.
