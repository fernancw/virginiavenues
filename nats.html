The Nats Stadium

Washington, D.C.

About Nationals Park

Opened in 2008, Nationals Park is the home of the Washington Nationals and one of the most modern ballparks on the East Coast. Located in the Capitol Riverfront neighborhood, it offers clean sightlines, strong fan energy, and easy access via the DC Metro.

Pros & Cons

Pros
  • Modern and clean stadium layout.
  • Convenient Metro access (Navy Yard–Ballpark station).
  • Good food variety featuring local DC vendors.
  • Nice riverfront atmosphere outside the stadium.
  • Strong energy during weekend games and events.
Cons
  • Concessions and tickets can be pricey.
  • Day games can be very hot depending on seating.
  • Parking is limited and expensive.
  • Metro crowds get heavy after games.
