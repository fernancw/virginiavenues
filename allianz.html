Allianz Amphitheater

Richmond, VA

About Allianz Amphitheater

The Allianz Amphitheater at Riverfront is a state-of-the-art outdoor venue located in downtown Richmond, along the James River near Historic Tredegar. Opened in 2025, this venue combines scenic riverfront views, modern facilities, and a focus on sustainability to host approximately 30 major concerts each season. With seating capacity for about 7,500 and a large lawn area for fans, Allianz is a key addition to Richmond’s growing music scene.

Pros & Cons

Pros
  • Beautiful riverfront setting with views of the James River.
  • Modern facilities designed for concerts and large events.
  • Capacity of about 7,500 fans with a mix of seating and lawn options.
  • Easy access to downtown Richmond, public transit, and rideshare.
  • Hosts a strong seasonal lineup of major touring artists.
Cons
  • Parking can be limited and requires walking or transit.
  • Limited shaded areas on the lawn during daytime events. (general for open-air venues)
  • Some neighborhood concerns over parking spillover.
  • Tickets for popular shows can sell out quickly due to relatively small capacity.
