Veterans United Home Loans Amphitheater

Virginia Beach, VA

About the Amphitheater

The Veterans United Home Loans Amphitheater is one of Virginia’s most popular outdoor concert venues, known for its large capacity, energetic summer shows, and open-air experience. Located just minutes from the Virginia Beach oceanfront, the venue attracts major touring artists and hosts a wide range of live performances from spring through fall.

Pros & Cons

Pros
  • Large outdoor venue with great summer concert atmosphere.
  • Good acoustics for an open-air amphitheater.
  • Wide variety of seating options, including lawn seating.
  • Strong lineup of major artists every season.
  • Easy to pair with a day trip to the beach or boardwalk.
Cons
  • Parking lots get extremely congested before and after shows.
  • Weather can heavily impact the experience (heat, rain, humidity).
  • Lawn seating can get crowded and muddy after storms.
  • Food and drinks are on the pricier side.
