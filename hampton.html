Hampton Coliseum

Hampton, VA

About the Hampton Coliseum

Opened in 1970, the Hampton Coliseum is one of Virginia’s most historic and recognizable entertainment venues. Known for its futuristic domed architecture and decades of iconic performances, the Coliseum regularly hosts concerts, festivals, sporting events, and community gatherings. With a capacity of around 9,700 and a rich musical legacy—including renowned annual jam band shows—the venue remains a cultural anchor of the Hampton Roads region.

Pros & Cons

Pros
  • Unique, iconic architecture with a strong historic identity.
  • Large, flexible indoor space suitable for concerts, festivals, and sporting events.
  • Decades-long reputation for hosting major touring artists and annual special events.
  • Central location in Hampton Roads with quick access to I-64.
  • Climate-controlled venue offering comfortable year-round shows.
Cons
  • Older infrastructure can feel dated compared to newer venues.
  • Concession offerings are limited during some events.
  • Parking lots can be slow to exit after high-attendance shows.
  • Acoustics vary depending on seating location within the dome.
  • Fewer nearby restaurant and nightlife options than downtown venues.
